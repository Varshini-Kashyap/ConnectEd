# ConnectEd — 2-Minute Demo Script

**Total target: ~2 minutes** (speak at a steady pace; actions in brackets.)

---

## Intro (≈15 sec)

*"ConnectEd helps GMU students connect in two ways: with peers for tutoring and study partners, and with alumni for career mentorship. I’ll walk you through the student experience—signup, profile, then both streams."*

---

## 1. Signup & questionnaire (≈35 sec)

*"First, I sign up as a student."*  
**[Sign up: email, password, name, role Student → Submit.]**

*"After signup I get the three-step questionnaire. Step one: major, minor, year. Step two: career goals, target companies, interests, skills, and what I’m looking for—tutoring, study partners, mentorship. Step three: review and submit."*  
**[Fill steps 1–2 briefly, submit on step 3.]**

*"That profile drives both peer matching and career matching later. Now I’m on the homepage."*

---

## 2. Homepage & Student stream (≈45 sec)

*"Homepage has two options: Student and Career. Student is for finding tutors and partners; we use an LLM so you can search in plain language—by interests like ‘swimming’ or by courses—and get relevant matches."*  
**[Click Find Tutors & Partners / Student.]**

*"Here’s the students list. I can post a short help request—e.g. ‘Need help with CS 310’—and the system matches tutors. I can also message a student directly."*  
**[Show list, post one short request, then open a conversation and send a quick message.]**

*"Messaging is in-app; you see the thread and can reply."*

---

## 3. Career stream & messaging (≈25 sec)

*"Back to home, then Career. Here we show alumni with a match percentage. That score is AI-driven from the profile we created—resume, interests, career goals—so you see who’s most relevant first."*  
**[Go to Career, show a few cards with match %.]**

*"I’ll connect with an alumnus and show messaging between student and alumni—same chat experience, but for career conversations."*  
**[Send connection request if needed, then open chat and send one message.]**

---

## Outro (≈5 sec)

*"So: one profile, two streams—Student for peers and tutoring, Career for alumni—with AI-powered search and matching and in-app messaging. Thanks."*

---

## Quick reference

| Section        | Say in one line |
|----------------|------------------|
| Intro          | Student + alumni; tutoring & career. |
| Signup         | Sign up as student. |
| Questionnaire  | 3 steps: major/year, goals & interests, review. Profile powers matching. |
| Home           | Two options: Student and Career. |
| Student        | LLM search by interests and courses; post requests; message students. |
| Career         | Match % from profile (resume, interests); connect and message alumni. |
| Outro          | One profile, two streams, AI matching, in-app messaging. |

---

*Tip: Rehearse once with a timer; trim the questionnaire or Career section if you run over.*
